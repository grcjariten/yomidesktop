# Start here

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder you can write to. Launch **YomiDesktop.exe** from that folder.
2. Click **Manage dictionaries** and import a Yomitan-compatible dictionary ZIP. The app starts with no dictionaries.
3. Point at Japanese in another window and press **Shift**. Press **Shift** again or **Esc** to close the popup.

This is an unsigned Windows x64 beta. The dictionary engine and local OCR do not require an account. AnkiConnect is optional. Your profile is stored in `%APPDATA%\YomiDesktop Public`.

YomiDesktop is an unofficial desktop app powered by Yomitan. See `LICENSE` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` for credits and licensing.
